Our 3 Search Methods

TF-IDF
Baseline — Keyword frequency matching using Term Frequency–Inverse Document Frequency and cosine similarity scoring.
Smart
Improved — Bi-gram TF-IDF with +0.20 skill-level boosting. Achieves 100% skill-match accuracy.
LSA
Semantic — Latent Semantic Analysis via SVD. Understands meaning beyond keywords, resolving the lexical gap.
